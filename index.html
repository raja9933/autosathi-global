Engine Help & Maintenance

1. Engine overheating

Stop safely if the temperature warning appears. Switch off the engine and let it cool. Never open a hot radiator cap. Ask a qualified mechanic to inspect the cooling system.

2. Low oil or unusual noise

Check the oil level according to the vehicle manual. If the oil warning light stays on or a severe knocking noise occurs, stop the engine and seek professional help.

3. Starting or battery problems

Look for loose or corroded terminals only when safe. Batteries can produce dangerous sparks. Follow the manual and use a trained technician if unsure.

Safety: These are general tips, not a diagnosis. Always follow the vehicle manufacturer's manual. Never work under an unsupported vehicle.

Vehicle Parts Guide

⚙️

Gearbox / Transmission

Transfers engine power and provides different gear ratios for driving. Symptoms such as gear slipping or unusual noise need proper inspection.

🔧

Differential

Allows driven wheels to rotate at different speeds while turning. Noise or oil leakage should be inspected by a mechanic.

⭕

Bearings

Support rotating parts and reduce friction. Select the correct size and specification for the exact vehicle model.

🛠️

Hydraulic system

Uses pressurised fluid to transmit force. Leaks or damaged hoses require professional attention; high-pressure fluid can cause serious injury.

Part fitment depends on the make, model, year and specification. Confirm compatibility before buying.
